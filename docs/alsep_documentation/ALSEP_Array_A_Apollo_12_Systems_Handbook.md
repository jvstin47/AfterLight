# ALSEP Array A Systems Handbook & Technical Reference

**Designation:** Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package (Array A)  
**Mission:** Apollo 12 (LM-6 Intrepid)  
**Deployment Date:** November 19, 1969  
**Final Deactivation:** September 30, 1977

## 2. Subsystem Specifications

### Central Station (CS)
- **Primary Function:** Acts as the communications, data handling, and power distribution nexus for all five scientific experiments.
- **Downlink Frequency:** 1712.5 MHz (S-band, Phase Modulated).
- **Transmitter Output:** 1.0 Watt nominal RF power.
- **Bitrate:** 1060 bps (PCM split-phase telemetry).
- **Thermal Design:** Top-mounted bimetallic actuators controlling open/shut aluminum radiator louvers to maintain interior electronics between -10°C and +50°C during the lunar day/night cycle (-180°C to +120°C ambient).

### SNAP-27 Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator (RTG)
- **Prime Contractor:** General Electric Space Division for the Atomic Energy Commission (AEC).
- **Fuel Capsule:** Plutonium-238 Dioxide (PuO2) encased in a high-strength superalloy capsule (approx. 44,500 Curies / 3.8 kg).
- **Thermal Output:** ~1,480 Watts thermal energy generated by radioactive alpha-decay.
- **Thermoelectric Conversion:** Lead-telluride (PbTe) thermocouples converting differential heat into 73.5 Watts of DC electrical power at 16 Volts.
- **Longevity:** Designed for 1 year of operation; operated without interruption for **7 years, 10 months** until NASA deactivated the network on September 30, 1977.

### Passive Seismic Experiment (PSE)
- **Sensor:** Three orthogonal long-period seismometers and one vertical short-period seismometer.
- **Sensitivity:** Capable of detecting lunar ground displacements as minute as 0.3 Angstroms (10^-10 meters).
- **Key Scientific Revelations:**
  - Discovered that the Moon has a layered internal structure: a 60-km crust, a rigid lithospheric mantle, and a partially molten deep asthenosphere/core.
  - Recorded thousands of deep moonquakes occurring at regular monthly intervals at depths of 800-1000 km, triggered by Earth's tidal gravitational pull during lunar perigee.

### Lunar Surface Magnetometer (LSM)
- **Sensors:** Three fluxgate sensors positioned on orthogonal 100-cm booms.
- **Discovery:** Detected a permanent local remanent magnetic field of 36 ± 5 gamma (nT), proving the ancient Moon possessed an active internal core dynamo billions of years ago.

### Solar Wind Spectrometer (SWS)
- **Configuration:** Seven modulated Faraday cups pointing in hemispherical directions to collect solar wind plasma.
- **Data:** Provided the first continuous, long-term direct measurements of interplanetary solar protons and alpha particles interacting directly with an unshielded planetary surface.

### Suprathermal Ion Detector Experiment (SIDE) & Cold Cathode Gauge (CCGE)
- **Sensors:** Velocity filter (Wien filter) and curved plate electrostatic analyzer measuring low-energy positive ions.
- **Cold Cathode Gauge:** Measured ambient daytime lunar atmospheric pressure at approximately 10^-12 torr (proving the Moon is surrounded by a collisionless exosphere rather than a true vacuum).
