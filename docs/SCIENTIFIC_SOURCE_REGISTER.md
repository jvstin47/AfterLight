**Project:** AFTERLIGHT — The Living Archival Monument  
**Document ID:** DOC-SSR-011  
**Policy:** Mandatory Primary Source Attribution, Discrepancy Reconciliation, and Evidence Transparency  
**Standard:** NASA NSSDC, NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS), LROC PDS, USGS, ISRO, CNSA, JAXA  

---

## 1. Editorial Integrity & Sourcing Protocol

AFTERLIGHT operates under strict academic sourcing guidelines:
1. **Primary Sources Prioritized:** Direct flight telemetry, mission reports, official post-flight engineering analyses, NASA/USGS publications, and LROC raw orbital data form the primary evidence base.
2. **Zero Fabrication Policy:** Hypothetical or plausible-sounding metadata is prohibited. When coordinates, masses, or outcomes are uncertain, the uncertainty is explicitly recorded.
3. **Discrepancy Preservation:** Where historical records disagree (e.g. Luna 9 landing coordinates prior to LROC candidate site identification), both values are recorded alongside their respective methodologies.
