# Apollo 12 EVA-2 Transcripts: The Surveyor 3 Rendezvous

**Date:** November 20, 1969  
**Source:** Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (ALSJ) / NASA History Division  
**Participants:**
- **Pete Conrad (CDR):** Commander (wearing red commander stripes on helmet)
- **Alan Bean (LMP):** Lunar Module Pilot
- **Ed Gibson (CAPCOM):** Capsule Communicator at Mission Control (Houston)

---

## Chronology of Key Moments at Surveyor Crater

### [134:40:15] Approaching the Crater Rim
> **Conrad:** "Hey, Al! Look across there! There it is! Surveyor 3, right where it's supposed to be!"  
> **Bean:** "Look at that! It looks like it's sitting right on the side of a hill, Pete. Boy, that's quite a crater!"  
> **Conrad:** "Look at how big it is. It's much bigger than it looked in the photographs. We're going to have to be careful walking down there."

### [134:48:20] Descending the Inner Slope
> **Conrad:** "The slope isn't bad here, Ed. I'd say it's about 10 or 12 degrees. Our boots sink in maybe 2 or 3 inches. Good footing. It doesn't feel slippery."  
> **Gibson (CAPCOM):** "Roger, Pete. Copy good footing on the slope."  
> **Bean:** "You know what strikes me? The spacecraft isn't bright white like the models. It's turned kind of a tan, brown color. It looks weathered."  
> **Conrad:** "Yeah! Look at the shadow side. It's got a brown coating all over it."

### [134:55:10] Inspecting the Touchdown Imprints
> **Bean:** "Look at the footpad marks here. You can see where it bounced! It hit here, bounced up, hit again, and then finally stopped down here."  
> **Conrad:** "Ed, we're looking at footpad 2. It's dug into the regolith maybe 2 inches, and the soil piled up on the rim of the pad is very fine-grained, velvety dark gray."

### [135:05:30] Cutting the Television Camera
> **Conrad:** "Okay, I've got the bolt cutters out. Let's see if we can get this TV camera free."  
> **Bean:** "Hold on, Pete. Let me get a stereo photo pair before you touch it."  
> **Conrad:** "Go ahead. Take the pictures. Man, these bolts are tight. Wait—there goes the first tube! Snapped clean!"  
> **Gibson (CAPCOM):** "Good cut, Pete."  
> **Conrad:** "That's two... and three! The camera's free! It's surprisingly light in one-sixth g, but I can feel the mass when I swing it."

### [135:22:45] The Sampling Scoop
> **Conrad:** "Now let's get the scoop. Al, grab the cutters and get that lower pin."  
> **Bean:** "Got it. The scoop bucket came off clean. It still has lunar dirt inside the jaws from 1967!"  
> **Conrad:** "Don't spill it! We want that 1967 dirt!"

---

## The Jettison of Equipment Before Liftoff

### [136:45:20] Discarding the Backpacks
> **Conrad:** "That's PLSS 1 and PLSS 2."  
> **Gibson (CAPCOM):** "Roger."  
> **Conrad:** "As a matter of fact, that may help you calibrate that thing [the Passive Seismic Experiment], because I got a clean kick on both of them. In other words, when they left the hatch, they departed the hatch and free-fell all the way to the ground without touching the ladder or anything on the way."  
> **Gibson (CAPCOM):** "Outstanding, Pete. We'll watch for the thump."
