## 1. Overview of GEOMETRIC (geometric-art.com)
GEOMETRIC is a browser-based generative art studio created by SHINICHI that transforms static imagery, motion video, and audio into geometric compositions using:
- **Vector Contours & Edge Density Detection:** Generating elevation isolines and topographic silhouettes from contrast and luminance gradients.
- **Node-and-Strut Networks:** Constellation-like point-cloud meshes connecting feature points with dynamic line segments.
- **Scanlines & Raster Waves:** Horizontal, vertical, or radial scanline passes with variable thickness, frequency modulation, and CRT-like raster distortion.
- **Audio-Reactive Deformation:** Modulating mesh vertex displacement and line weights via sound frequencies.

## 2. Application to AFTERLIGHT's Visual Direction

AFTERLIGHT's core direction is a **hybrid of scientific authenticity and artistic presentation**. Geometric art techniques directly support the archival and storytelling goals:

### A. Archival Telemetry & Vidicon Raster Simulation
- **Surveyor 3 Slow-Scan TV Aesthetics:** Surveyor 3 utilized a 200-line and 600-line Vidicon slow-scan camera format transmitted via S-band. Scanline and raster generation can recreate the scan-sweep effect of original 1967 television frames without obscuring photographic detail.
- **LM Landing Radar & Guidance Computer (LGC):** The TRW descent radar and Ryan landing radar used frequency-modulated continuous wave (FMCW) doppler beams. Geometric node webs can represent radar altitude sampling cones touching down onto the regolith.

### B. Topographic & Contour Cartography
- **Surveyor Crater Micro-Topography:** Transforming LRO NAC elevation data (DTM) into layered vector contour lines (isobaths and isolines), highlighting the 11° to 14° slope upon which Surveyor 3 rested.
- **Astronaut Footstep Path Densities:** Visualizing the high-traffic zones between Intrepid, the ALSEP array, and the crater chain through node density heatmaps.

### C. Technical Blueprint & Ghosted Holographic Overlays
- **The \"Machines That Stayed Behind\" Visual Metaphor:** When exploring the abandoned hardware in future interactive phases, switching into a vector wireframe/geometric contour mode can reveal internal subsystems (e.g. the fuel tanks within Intrepid's gold foil, or the Plutonium-238 capsule inside the SNAP-27 RTG) as glowing geometric outlines against the dark lunar void.
- **Seismic Waveform Resonance:** The ALSEP Passive Seismic Experiment recorded the Moon vibrating for over an hour following the ascent stage crash. Audio-reactive vector waves can translate those long-period seismic frequencies into animated geometric contours on the surface.
